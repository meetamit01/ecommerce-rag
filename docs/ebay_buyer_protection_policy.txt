EBAY BUYER PROTECTION & RETURN POLICY
Source: eBay Help Center (https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/returns-refunds/returns-refunds?id=4008)
Last Updated: 2024

=== OVERVIEW ===
eBay offers comprehensive buyer protection through its Money Back Guarantee program. The return and refund experience depends on whether the seller accepts returns and the specific terms of their listing.

=== EBAY MONEY BACK GUARANTEE ===
eBay's Money Back Guarantee covers buyers in the following situations:
- The item is not received
- The item does not match the listing description
- The item arrives damaged or defective
- The item is not authentic (counterfeit)

Coverage Period: Claims must be filed within 30 days of the actual or estimated delivery date.

=== SELLER RETURN POLICIES ===
eBay sellers can set their own return policies, which may include:

1. NO RETURNS ACCEPTED
   - The seller does not accept returns for change of mind.
   - However, eBay's Money Back Guarantee still applies for "item not as described" cases.

2. 30-DAY RETURNS
   - Buyer has 30 days from delivery to initiate a return.
   - Seller may specify who pays return shipping.

3. 60-DAY RETURNS
   - Extended return window of 60 days.
   - Common for sellers with eBay Top Rated Plus status.

4. FREE RETURNS
   - Seller offers free return shipping on all returns.
   - Often offered by Top Rated Sellers to qualify for Top Rated Plus badge.

=== ITEM NOT AS DESCRIBED ===
If an item does not match the listing:
- Buyer must report the issue within 30 days of delivery
- Seller has 3 business days to respond
- If seller does not respond, eBay will step in
- eBay may issue a full refund including original shipping
- Return shipping is covered by eBay or the seller in most cases

=== CHANGE OF MIND RETURNS ===
If buyer simply changes their mind:
- Return is only possible if seller's policy allows it
- Buyer typically pays return shipping
- Some sellers charge a restocking fee (must be disclosed in listing)
- Restocking fees cannot exceed 15% of the item price per eBay policy

=== RETURN PROCESS ===
Step 1: Go to "Purchase History" in your eBay account
Step 2: Find the item and click "Return this item"
Step 3: Select the reason for return (damaged, not as described, changed mind, etc.)
Step 4: Add photos if item is damaged or defective
Step 5: Submit the return request
Step 6: Wait for seller to respond (3 business days)
Step 7: If approved, ship the item back
Step 8: Refund is issued after seller receives item

=== REFUND TIMELINES ===
- PayPal: 3 to 5 business days
- Credit/Debit Card: 3 to 10 business days
- eBay Gift Card: Within 1 business day
- Checks: Up to 10 business days

=== DISCOUNTED AND AUCTION ITEMS ===
- Items purchased at auction (bidding) follow the seller's stated return policy.
- Items listed as "Final Sale" or "As Is" are NOT eligible for returns based on buyer preference.
- However, "Item Not As Described" protection still applies even for Final Sale listings.
- Discounted Buy-It-Now items follow the seller's standard return policy.

=== HIGH-VALUE ITEMS ===
For items over $750:
- Signature confirmation is required for delivery
- eBay may request additional documentation for returns
- Authenticity Guarantee program applies to certain high-value items (watches, sneakers, trading cards, handbags)

=== AUTHENTICITY GUARANTEE ===
eBay's Authenticity Guarantee covers:
- Sneakers over $100
- Watches over $500
- Trading cards over $250
- Handbags and accessories over $500
- Fine jewelry over $500
Items are inspected by eBay's third-party authenticators before delivery.
If the item fails authentication upon return, the buyer gets a full refund.

=== INTERNATIONAL PURCHASES ===
- International returns may be subject to import duties and taxes
- eBay's Global Shipping Program simplifies international returns
- Return shipping for international orders may be more expensive
- Currency exchange rates apply to refunds for international transactions

=== BUYER RESPONSIBILITIES ===
Buyers must:
- Return items in the same condition as received
- Use tracked shipping for returns over $750
- Pack items securely to prevent damage during return shipping
- Not return items that have been used beyond what is necessary to assess them

=== SELLER RESPONSIBILITIES ===
Sellers must:
- Respond to return requests within 3 business days
- Issue refunds within 2 business days of receiving the returned item
- Not threaten buyers for leaving negative feedback
- Comply with eBay's return policy even if their listing says "no returns"

=== CONTACT EBAY ===
If a return dispute cannot be resolved with the seller, eBay Customer Support can be reached:
- Through the Resolution Center in your eBay account
- Via chat or phone support
- eBay will review the case and make a final decision within 48 hours
