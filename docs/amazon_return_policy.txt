AMAZON RETURN POLICY - COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE
Source: Amazon Customer Service (https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7)
Last Updated: 2024

=== OVERVIEW ===
Amazon offers a 30-day return policy for most items sold and fulfilled by Amazon. Items must be returned in their original condition. The return window begins from the date of delivery.

=== RETURN WINDOW ===
- Standard Items: 30 days from delivery date
- Electronics (Televisions, Computers): 30 days from delivery
- Baby Items: 90 days from delivery
- Amazon Devices (Kindle, Echo, Fire TV): 30 days from delivery
- Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods groceries: 7 days from delivery
- Hazardous Materials: Non-returnable
- Holiday Items: Extended return window — items purchased between November 1 and December 31 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

=== RETURN CONDITIONS ===
Items must be:
- Returned in original condition (unused, unworn, or undamaged)
- Packaged in original packaging or appropriate packaging
- Accompanied by all original accessories and documentation
- Have original tags still attached (for apparel/shoes)

=== NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS ===
The following categories CANNOT be returned:
- Hazardous materials (flammable liquids, gases)
- Items sold by third-party sellers that are marked as non-returnable
- Downloadable software and digital content
- Gift cards and prepaid game cards
- Online subscriptions after they have been accessed
- Computer laptops, desktops, and Kindles (after 30 days)
- Items that have been used, damaged, or are missing parts
- Customized or personalized products
- Live insects/plants

=== REFUND METHODS ===
Amazon provides refunds via:
1. Original payment method (credit/debit card) — 3 to 5 business days
2. Amazon Gift Card balance — Instant
3. Check (for cash payments) — 10 business days

=== RETURN SHIPPING ===
- Free return shipping is available for most items sold by Amazon
- Items that are damaged or defective: Amazon covers the return shipping cost
- Change of mind returns: Buyer may be responsible for return shipping cost
- Third-party seller returns: Return shipping policy may vary by seller

=== DISCOUNTED AND SALE ITEMS ===
- Items purchased at a discount or during a sale ARE eligible for return within the standard return window.
- Final Sale items are explicitly marked as "non-returnable" at the time of purchase.
- Items purchased using promotional credits are eligible for return; however, the promotional credit will NOT be refunded.
- Lightning Deals: Eligible for return within standard return window unless marked otherwise.

=== AMAZON PRIME RETURNS ===
- Amazon Prime members have the same 30-day return window.
- Prime members enjoy FREE return shipping on eligible items.
- Some items may qualify for instant refunds (refund is processed before the item is returned).

=== THIRD-PARTY SELLER RETURNS ===
- Third-party sellers on Amazon Marketplace may have different return policies.
- Sellers must offer a return policy that is at least as good as Amazon's standard return policy.
- If a seller refuses a valid return, Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee may cover the buyer.
- You can request a return directly through the seller or via Amazon's return portal.

=== A-TO-Z GUARANTEE ===
Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee protects customers when buying from third-party sellers:
- Item not received within 3 days after the latest estimated delivery date
- Item is materially different from what was ordered
- Return is refused for a reason not covered by seller's policy
- Refund not issued within 2 business days of return receipt

=== HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN ===
Step 1: Go to "Returns & Orders" in your account
Step 2: Find the order and click "Return or Replace Items"
Step 3: Select the reason for return
Step 4: Choose refund or replacement option
Step 5: Print the prepaid return label (if applicable)
Step 6: Drop off the package at the designated carrier location
Step 7: Track your return and refund status in "Your Orders"

=== EXCHANGES ===
- Amazon does not offer direct exchanges in most cases.
- To exchange an item, return the original and place a new order.
- In some cases, a replacement of the same item can be requested if the item is defective.

=== INTERNATIONAL ORDERS ===
- International returns may be subject to customs duties and import fees.
- Return windows may vary for international orders.
- Contact Amazon Customer Service for international return instructions.

=== GIFT RETURNS ===
- If you received an item as a gift, you can return it for an Amazon Gift Card balance.
- The gift giver will NOT be notified of the return.
- You will need the order number or gift receipt to initiate a gift return.
