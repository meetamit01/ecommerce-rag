BEST BUY RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY
Source: Best Buy Customer Service (https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014)
Last Updated: 2024

=== OVERVIEW ===
Best Buy accepts returns and exchanges on most products within 15 days of purchase. My Best Buy Plus and Total members receive extended return windows.

=== STANDARD RETURN WINDOW (Non-Members) ===
- Most products: 15 days from date of purchase
- Major appliances: 15 days
- Cell phones and mobile devices: 15 days (must be undamaged with original packaging)
- Non-member deadline for holiday purchases: January 13 (for items purchased November 1–December 31)

=== MY BEST BUY PLUS & TOTAL MEMBER RETURN WINDOWS ===
- My Best Buy Plus Members: 30-day return window on most products
- My Best Buy Total Members: 60-day return window on most products
- Members also receive extended holiday return window until February 13

=== RETURN REQUIREMENTS ===
To make a return, customers must bring:
1. The product in its original packaging (if possible)
2. All original accessories, cables, manuals, and included items
3. Receipt or proof of purchase (packing slip for online orders)
4. Valid government-issued ID (for select returns)
5. Original payment method (credit/debit card)

=== NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS ===
The following CANNOT be returned to Best Buy:
- Consumable items (batteries, ink cartridges) — once opened
- Prepaid cards (phone, gaming, gift cards)
- Opened computer software, video games, DVDs, Blu-Rays (exchange for same title only)
- Digital content (downloads, digital subscriptions)
- Items that have been damaged by the customer
- Final Sale items (clearly marked on receipt and at time of purchase)
- Customized or built-to-order computers
- Contracts (mobile carrier contracts — handled by carrier directly)

=== ELECTRONICS RETURN POLICY ===
- Laptops and computers: 15 days (30 days for Plus, 60 days for Total)
- Tablets: 15 days
- Smartphones and smartwatches: 15 days (device must be factory reset)
- Televisions (under 42"): 15 days
- Televisions (42" and above): 15 days; requires original packaging or Best Buy packaging service
- Gaming consoles: 15 days
- Cameras and camcorders: 15 days (must be in original packaging)
- Drones: 15 days; must include all parts and accessories

=== MAJOR APPLIANCES RETURN POLICY ===
- 15 days for all major appliances (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges)
- Appliances must be in original condition (not installed or used beyond initial testing)
- Delivery and installation fees are non-refundable once service is performed
- Special order appliances: Subject to 15% restocking fee

=== OPENED ITEMS ===
- Most opened items ARE eligible for return within the return window
- Items must be complete with all parts, accessories, and original packaging
- For heavily used items, Best Buy reserves the right to deny the return or charge a restocking fee
- Restocking fee: Up to 15% of purchase price at Best Buy's discretion

=== FINAL SALE AND "AS IS" ITEMS ===
- Final Sale items are marked clearly and cannot be returned or exchanged
- Open-box items may have shorter return windows — check the tag/label at time of purchase
- "As Is" items cannot be returned — sold in current condition

=== HOLIDAY RETURN POLICY ===
- Items purchased from November 1 through December 31 can be returned through January 13 (non-members) or February 13 (Plus/Total members)
- Standard return restrictions still apply (non-returnable item categories remain excluded)
- Electronics and appliances still subject to 15-day return window from purchase date

=== DISCOUNTED ITEMS AND SALE PRICES ===
- Items purchased on sale or at a discounted price are eligible for return within the standard return window
- Sale price is honored at time of purchase; no price matching on returned items
- If an item goes on sale after you purchase it, Best Buy offers price matching within 15 days of purchase (with price match guarantee)
- Price match must be for identical model from a qualifying competitor

=== GEEK SQUAD PROTECTION PLANS ===
Best Buy's Geek Squad offers protection plans:
- Geek Squad Protection (Standard): Covers mechanical and electrical failures
- Geek Squad Protection (Accidental Damage): Covers drops, spills, and accidents
- Claim process: Visit any Best Buy store or GeekSquad.com/claims
- Most claims resolved within 14 business days (repair or replacement)
- Plans transferable if item is sold or gifted
- Cancel within 30 days for full refund; prorated refund after 30 days

=== HOW TO RETURN AN ITEM ===

IN STORE:
1. Bring item, receipt, and original packaging to any Best Buy store
2. Go to the Customer Service or Returns counter
3. Present ID if required
4. Receive refund to original payment method or store credit

BY MAIL (Online Purchases):
1. Log into BestBuy.com → Orders → Return Items
2. Select item(s) to return and reason
3. Print prepaid return label (free for defective items; $7.99 for change-of-mind returns)
4. Pack item securely with all original accessories
5. Drop off at UPS or FedEx location
6. Refund processed within 7–10 business days after receipt

GEEK SQUAD SERVICES:
- Geek Squad can assist with complex returns or exchanges involving technical issues
- Available in-store at the Geek Squad counter
- Home pickup available for large items (fee may apply)

=== REFUND TIMELINE ===
- Credit Card: 5–7 business days
- Debit Card: 5–7 business days
- Best Buy Gift Card: Immediate (issued as Best Buy Gift Card)
- Cash: Immediate (in-store)
- PayPal: 3–5 business days
- Financing (Best Buy Credit Card / Citi): 5–7 business days

=== TRADE-IN PROGRAM ===
Best Buy accepts trade-ins for:
- Smartphones, tablets, computers
- Gaming consoles and games
- Wearables
Trade-in value can be applied toward a new purchase or issued as a Best Buy Gift Card.
Trade-in values estimated at: BestBuy.com/trade-in
