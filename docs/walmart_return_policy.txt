WALMART RETURN POLICY & CUSTOMER SERVICE GUIDE
Source: Walmart Help Center (https://www.walmart.com/help/article/walmart-s-return-policy/cc193fe2b1ef4704893c35b23b820a13)
Last Updated: 2024

=== OVERVIEW ===
Walmart offers flexible return policies across its stores and Walmart.com. Most items purchased from Walmart can be returned or exchanged within 90 days of purchase with a receipt.

=== STANDARD RETURN WINDOW ===
Walmart's standard return window is 90 days from the date of purchase for most items.
- Electronics: 30 days
- Computers, laptops, tablets: 15 days
- Cell phones (prepaid and contract): 14 days
- eBook readers: 15 days
- Opened movies, music, video games: Exchange for same title only
- Grocery items: Return within 90 days for most items (non-perishables)
- Perishable food: Return within 90 days with receipt

=== EXTENDED RETURN WINDOW - WALMART+ MEMBERS ===
Walmart+ members enjoy extended return benefits:
- Extended holiday return window: Items purchased October 1 through December 31 can be returned until January 31 of the following year
- Free returns via mail for online orders
- No need to visit a store for returns

=== NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS AT WALMART ===
The following items are non-returnable:
- Firearms, ammunition, and crossbows
- Air guns and BB guns
- Pepper spray
- Prescription medications
- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- Alcohol (where prohibited by state law)
- Cigarettes and tobacco products
- Trading cards and sports trading cards (opened)
- Items sold by select marketplace sellers (marked as non-returnable)

=== ITEMS THAT CAN ONLY BE EXCHANGED ===
- Opened music, movies, software, and video games: Can only be exchanged for the exact same title
- Trading cards: Cannot be returned or exchanged once opened

=== RETURN OPTIONS ===
Customers can return items in three ways:

1. IN-STORE RETURNS
   - Bring item and receipt (or order confirmation email) to any Walmart store
   - No receipt? Returns accepted with government-issued ID (up to 3 times per year)
   - Customer Service desk processes returns during store hours
   - Refund method: Original payment method or store credit

2. BY MAIL (ONLINE PURCHASES)
   - Go to Walmart.com account → Order History → Return Items
   - Select items to return and reason for return
   - Print prepaid return label (free for most items)
   - Drop off at FedEx or USPS location
   - Refund processed within 10 days of return receipt

3. FREE PICKUP (WALMART+ MEMBERS)
   - Schedule a pickup from your home via Walmart+ app
   - Carrier picks up return package
   - No box or label needed in some cases

=== REFUND METHODS ===
- Credit/Debit Card: 3–5 business days
- Walmart Gift Card: Immediate
- Check (original payment): 10 business days
- Cash (original payment was cash): Immediate in-store
- PayPal: 3–5 business days

=== RETURN POLICY FOR MARKETPLACE SELLERS ===
Walmart Marketplace sellers (third-party) may have different policies:
- Sellers set their own return windows (minimum 30 days)
- Walmart's Marketplace Guarantee covers buyers if sellers do not comply
- Buyers can request Walmart to step in after attempting to resolve with seller
- Walmart may issue a refund of up to $1,000 per transaction under the Marketplace Guarantee

=== DISCOUNTED AND CLEARANCE ITEMS ===
- Regular clearance items ARE eligible for return within the standard 90-day window.
- "Final Clearance" or "As Is" items are clearly marked and are NOT eligible for return.
- Items marked with a yellow clearance sticker can be returned within 90 days.
- Rollback priced items: Full return policy applies.
- Items purchased with Walmart Cash or Walmart Rewards: Refunds issued as Walmart Cash (not cash or card credit).

=== HOLIDAY RETURN POLICY ===
- Items purchased October 1 through December 31 are eligible for return until January 31 of the following year.
- This applies to most general merchandise categories.
- Electronics purchased during holiday season still follow the standard 30-day return window.

=== DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS ===
For items that arrive damaged or defective:
- Report within 90 days of purchase (or 30 days for electronics)
- Return in-store with the damaged item
- For online orders: Contact Walmart.com support for immediate resolution
- Replacement shipped at no charge, or full refund issued
- Walmart may not require you to return the damaged item for low-value orders

=== RECEIPTS & PROOF OF PURCHASE ===
Returns with receipt:
- Full refund to original payment method
- No limitations

Returns without receipt:
- Valid government-issued ID required
- Non-receipt returns limited to $50 cash refund
- Beyond $50: Store credit (Walmart Gift Card) issued
- Limit of 3 non-receipt returns per year per customer
- Manager approval required for non-receipt returns over $25

=== THIRD-PARTY WARRANTIES ===
- Walmart sells Walmart Protection Plans (extended warranties) through Asurion
- Protection Plans cover accidental damage, mechanical failures, and 24/7 tech support
- Claims can be filed at: walmart.com/protection or 1-877-968-6391
- Most claims resolved within 5 business days (repair or replacement)

=== EXCHANGE POLICY ===
- Most items can be exchanged for the same or different item
- Exchanges processed at Customer Service desk in-store
- Online exchanges require returning the original item and placing a new order
- Exchanges follow the same timeline as returns (90 days, or category-specific)

=== PRESCRIPTION DRUG & PHARMACY POLICY ===
- Prescription medications cannot be returned due to safety and legal regulations
- Unused, sealed OTC (over-the-counter) medications CAN be returned within 90 days with receipt
- Pharmacy billing errors: Contact the pharmacy directly

=== CONTACT WALMART CUSTOMER SERVICE ===
- Phone: 1-800-WALMART (1-800-925-6278)
- Website Chat: walmart.com/help (24/7 automated; live agents during business hours)
- Store hours: Varies by location (most stores open 6AM–11PM)
- Online chat hours: Monday–Sunday, 8AM–11PM EST
