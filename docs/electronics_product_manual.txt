SECTION 1: WIRELESS EARBUDS - PRODUCT MANUAL
=========================================================

PRODUCT NAME: ProSound Wireless Earbuds Model WE-500
WARRANTY PERIOD: 1 Year Limited Warranty

--- PACKAGE CONTENTS ---
- 2x Earbud units (Left & Right)
- 1x Charging Case
- 3x Ear tip sizes (Small, Medium, Large)
- 1x USB-C Charging Cable
- 1x Quick Start Guide
- 1x Safety Information Booklet

--- SPECIFICATIONS ---
- Driver Size: 10mm dynamic driver
- Frequency Response: 20Hz – 20,000Hz
- Battery Life: 8 hours (earbuds) + 24 hours (charging case)
- Charging Time: 1.5 hours (earbuds), 2 hours (case)
- Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3
- Range: Up to 10 meters (33 feet)
- Water Resistance: IPX4 (splash-proof)
- Weight: 5g per earbud
- Supported Codecs: SBC, AAC, aptX

--- INITIAL SETUP ---
1. Remove earbuds from charging case — they will automatically enter pairing mode (LED flashes blue rapidly)
2. On your device, navigate to Bluetooth settings
3. Select "ProSound WE-500" from the available devices list
4. Wait for the confirmation sound ("Connected")
5. For Android: Enable "HD Audio" in Bluetooth settings for best quality
6. For iOS: Earbuds are automatically set to AAC codec

--- TOUCH CONTROLS ---
Left Earbud:
- Single tap: Play/Pause
- Double tap: Previous track
- Triple tap: Volume down
- Long press (2 sec): Activate voice assistant

Right Earbud:
- Single tap: Play/Pause
- Double tap: Next track
- Triple tap: Volume up
- Long press (2 sec): Toggle ANC (Active Noise Cancellation)

--- CHARGING ---
- Place earbuds in case, ensure they click into position
- Connect USB-C cable to case charging port
- LED indicator: Red = charging, Green = fully charged
- Wireless charging: Compatible with Qi wireless chargers (case only)
- DO NOT use charging voltages above 5V/1A

--- TROUBLESHOOTING ---
Problem: Earbuds not connecting
Solution:
  1. Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your device
  2. Place earbuds back in case for 10 seconds, then remove
  3. If still not connecting, hold both earbuds for 10 seconds to factory reset
  4. Re-pair as a new device

Problem: One earbud has lower volume
Solution:
  1. Clean the speaker mesh with a dry cotton swab
  2. Check audio balance settings on your device
  3. Reset earbuds to factory settings

Problem: Battery draining fast
Solution:
  1. Disable ANC when not needed
  2. Reduce volume level
  3. Ensure firmware is up to date via the companion app

Problem: Poor call quality
Solution:
  1. Ensure microphone is not covered by ear tip
  2. Move away from interference sources (WiFi routers, microwaves)
  3. Clean microphone port gently

--- WARRANTY INFORMATION (EARBUDS) ---
- 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects
- Warranty does NOT cover:
  * Physical damage (drops, cracks)
  * Water damage beyond IPX4 rating
  * Ear tip wear and tear
  * Damage from use with unauthorized charging accessories
- Warranty claim: Contact support with proof of purchase

SECTION 2: SMARTWATCH - PRODUCT MANUAL
=========================================================

PRODUCT NAME: FitPro Smartwatch Model SW-300
WARRANTY PERIOD: 1 Year Limited Warranty

--- SPECIFICATIONS ---
- Display: 1.4" AMOLED, 320x320 resolution
- Battery Life: 7 days (normal use), 3 days (GPS active)
- Water Resistance: 5ATM (50 meters depth)
- Sensors: Heart Rate, SpO2, Accelerometer, Gyroscope, GPS
- Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.0, Wi-Fi 2.4GHz
- Compatibility: iOS 12+, Android 8.0+
- Storage: 4GB internal (music storage)
- Charging: Magnetic charging cable (included)

--- HEALTH FEATURES ---
1. Heart Rate Monitoring
   - 24/7 continuous monitoring
   - Alerts for abnormal heart rate (above 120 BPM at rest or below 40 BPM)
   - NOTE: This device is NOT a medical device. Consult a doctor for health concerns.

2. Blood Oxygen (SpO2)
   - Measures blood oxygen saturation
   - Normal range: 95–100%
   - Measurement takes approximately 30 seconds
   - NOT intended for medical diagnosis

3. Sleep Tracking
   - Automatically detects sleep stages (light, deep, REM)
   - Tracks sleep duration and quality score
   - Wear the watch on your non-dominant wrist for accurate sleep tracking

4. Stress Monitoring
   - Uses HRV (Heart Rate Variability) to estimate stress levels
   - Guided breathing exercises available through the FitPro app

--- GPS USAGE ---
- First GPS lock may take 2–3 minutes in open areas
- GPS accuracy: ±5 meters in open sky
- Assisted GPS (A-GPS): Downloads satellite data via Wi-Fi for faster lock
- Reduce GPS usage to extend battery life

--- TROUBLESHOOTING (SMARTWATCH) ---
Problem: Watch not syncing with phone
Solution:
  1. Ensure Bluetooth is on and watch is within range
  2. Force close and reopen the FitPro app
  3. Restart both the watch and phone
  4. Unpair and re-pair the watch in Bluetooth settings

Problem: Heart rate readings inaccurate
Solution:
  1. Wear watch snug on wrist (not too loose)
  2. Keep the sensor and wrist clean and dry
  3. Allow 30 seconds for the reading to stabilize
  4. Avoid movement during manual readings

Problem: GPS not working
Solution:
  1. Move to an open area with clear sky view
  2. Enable Location permissions for FitPro app
  3. Sync A-GPS data via the app (requires Wi-Fi)
