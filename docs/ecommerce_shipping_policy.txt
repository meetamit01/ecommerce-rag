E-COMMERCE SHIPPING POLICY & DELIVERY GUIDELINES
Source: Standard E-Commerce Shipping Policy (Based on Shopify Public Documentation and Industry Standards)
Reference: https://www.shopify.com/blog/shipping-policy
Last Updated: 2024

=== OVERVIEW ===
This document outlines standard e-commerce shipping policies, delivery timelines, costs, and related information for online retail purchases. These policies represent industry-standard practices across major e-commerce platforms.

=== SHIPPING METHODS & TIMELINES ===

1. STANDARD SHIPPING
   - Estimated Delivery: 5–7 business days
   - Cost: Free on orders over $50; $5.99 for orders under $50
   - Carrier: USPS, FedEx Ground, or UPS Ground
   - Tracking: Provided via email upon shipment

2. EXPEDITED SHIPPING
   - Estimated Delivery: 2–3 business days
   - Cost: $12.99 flat rate
   - Carrier: FedEx Express or UPS 2-Day
   - Cutoff time: Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST ship same day

3. OVERNIGHT / NEXT-DAY SHIPPING
   - Estimated Delivery: Next business day
   - Cost: $24.99 flat rate
   - Carrier: FedEx Priority Overnight or UPS Next Day Air
   - Cutoff time: Orders placed before 12:00 PM EST ship same day
   - Not available on weekends or federal holidays

4. SAME-DAY DELIVERY (SELECT CITIES)
   - Estimated Delivery: Within 4–6 hours of order placement
   - Cost: $14.99
   - Available cities: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix
   - Orders must be placed before 12:00 PM local time
   - Delivered via DoorDash Drive or Instacart

5. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
   - Estimated Delivery: 10–21 business days
   - Cost: Calculated at checkout based on destination country and package weight
   - Carrier: DHL Express, FedEx International, or USPS First-Class International
   - Customs duties and taxes: Buyer's responsibility
   - Restricted countries: North Korea, Iran, Cuba, Syria, Russia (as per U.S. export regulations)

=== FREE SHIPPING ELIGIBILITY ===
Free standard shipping is available on:
- Orders totaling $50 or more (after discounts/coupons)
- Orders placed by loyalty program members (Silver tier and above) — no minimum order
- Subscription box orders (all subscription tiers)
- Exchange orders for defective or damaged items

Note: Free shipping applies to the contiguous United States only. Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and U.S. territories may incur additional shipping surcharges.

=== ORDER PROCESSING TIME ===
- Standard orders: Processed within 1–2 business days
- Custom or personalized orders: 3–5 business days before shipping
- Pre-order items: Shipped on the listed release date
- Orders placed on weekends/holidays: Processed on the next business day
- During peak seasons (Black Friday, Christmas): Processing may take 3–4 business days

=== TRACKING YOUR ORDER ===
- A shipping confirmation email with tracking number is sent within 24 hours of shipment
- Track your order at: www.store.com/track-order or via the carrier's website
- Tracking updates may take 24–48 hours after the tracking number is generated
- SMS tracking alerts available — opt in during checkout or in account settings

=== SHIPPING ADDRESS POLICY ===
- Orders can be shipped to most addresses in the United States and internationally
- P.O. Boxes: Accepted for USPS standard shipping only (not for FedEx or UPS)
- APO/FPO Military Addresses: Accepted via USPS Priority Mail (7–21 business days)
- Address changes: Must be requested within 1 hour of order placement by contacting customer support
- Incorrect address: Customer is responsible for costs incurred due to incorrect shipping address

=== LOST OR STOLEN PACKAGES ===
If your package is marked as "Delivered" but not received:
1. Check with neighbors and in mailbox/porch areas
2. Wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes mark early)
3. Contact carrier with tracking number to file a trace
4. Contact our customer support if package not located within 3 business days
5. We will file a claim with the carrier on your behalf
6. Resolution: Replacement shipment or refund within 5–7 business days after investigation

For packages stolen after delivery (porch piracy):
- File a police report
- Contact your homeowner's or renter's insurance
- We may offer a one-time courtesy replacement at our discretion

=== DAMAGED PACKAGES ===
If your package arrives damaged:
1. Document damage with photos before opening (if possible)
2. Photograph the damaged item(s) and packaging
3. Contact customer support within 72 hours of delivery
4. Email photos to: shipping-claims@store.com
5. A replacement will be shipped at no charge or a full refund issued within 3–5 business days

=== SHIPPING RESTRICTIONS ===
Certain items have shipping restrictions:
- Hazardous materials (lithium batteries over certain size, aerosols): Ground shipping only
- Alcohol: Available only in select states; requires age verification upon delivery
- Prescription items: Require valid prescription and ship only to confirmed address
- Live plants: Shipped only to continental U.S.; not available during extreme weather
- Oversized items (over 150 lbs or 108" in length): Freight shipping, contact for quote

=== HOLIDAY SHIPPING DEADLINES ===
To guarantee delivery by Christmas (December 25):
- Standard Shipping: Order by December 15
- Expedited Shipping: Order by December 20
- Overnight Shipping: Order by December 23 (before 12 PM EST)

=== SHIPPING FOR RETURNS ===
- Prepaid return labels are provided for defective or damaged items (free)
- Change-of-mind returns: Customer pays return shipping (flat rate $6.99 label available)
- Exchanges: Free return shipping for size/color exchanges
- Return drop-off: Available at partner locations (Walgreens, FedEx Office locations)

=== SUBSCRIPTION ORDERS ===
- Subscription boxes ship on the 1st of each month
- Subscribers can pause, skip, or cancel via their account dashboard
- Cancellations must be made 5 days before the next billing date to avoid shipping the next box
- Subscription shipping is always free, regardless of order value

=== ENVIRONMENTAL COMMITMENT ===
- All packaging uses at least 80% recycled materials
- Packing peanuts replaced with biodegradable cornstarch alternatives
- Opt for "Ship in Fewer Boxes" option at checkout to reduce packaging waste
- Carbon-neutral shipping option available (adds $0.50 per order) — offsets carbon footprint through certified programs
